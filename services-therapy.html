What We Offer

Therapy Services

Individualized intervention plans built around each child, drawing on evidence-based approaches to build skills, ease behaviors, and support everyday life.

🌱

ABA Therapy

Applied Behavior Analysis, a structured, evidence-based approach that builds skills step by step and reduces behaviors that get in the way of learning.

Children
🤝

Social Skills Training

Guided practice in turn-taking, conversation, and peer interaction to help children build confidence in social settings.

Children
💭

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Age-appropriate CBT techniques to help children understand and manage anxious thoughts, worries, and emotional reactions.

Children
🧸

Play Therapy

Using play as a natural language for younger children to process emotions, build coping skills, and express what's hard to put into words.

Children
🎵

Music Therapy

Rhythm and sound-based sessions that support communication, emotional regulation, and sensory engagement.

Children
📐

Behavior Modification

Practical, consistent strategies for home and school that reduce challenging behaviors and reinforce positive ones.

Children
