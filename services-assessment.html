What We Offer

Psychological Assessment

A clear, evidence-based starting point. Before any plan is made, we take time to understand what's actually going on, so therapy and support are built around real findings, not guesswork.

🧩

Autism Spectrum Assessment

A structured evaluation of social communication, behavior, and developmental history to identify Autism Spectrum traits and guide next steps.

Children
🧠

Intellectual Disability Assessment

Cognitive and adaptive functioning evaluation to understand a child's learning profile and day-to-day support needs.

Children
⚡

ADHD Assessment

Evaluation of attention, focus, and activity levels across home and school settings to reach a clear, evidence-based picture.

Children
📖

Learning Disability Assessment

Identifies specific difficulties with reading, writing, or numeracy so schools and families can put the right support in place.

Children
🧭

Behavior Problems Assessment

A closer look at challenging or concerning behaviors, their triggers, and patterns, to inform a targeted, practical intervention plan.

Children
🤍

Neglect Evaluation

A sensitive, professional evaluation to assess the emotional and developmental impact of neglect, conducted with care and confidentiality.
